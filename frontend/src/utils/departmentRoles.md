# Department Importance and Workflow Mapping

## Key Departments
- **Engineering**
- **Operational**
- **General Affair (GA)**

## Workflow usage per department
1. **Operational**
   - Uses **Work Orders (WO)** and **checklists**.
   - **Daily checklist** is their primary tool.
2. **Engineering**
   - Uses **Work Orders (WO)** and **checklists**.
   - Critical role in **Asset Maintenance**.
3. **General Affair (GA)**
   - Uses **Work Orders (WO)** and **checklists**.
   - Primarily focuses on **Asset Management**.

These definitions should guide future feature development, role‑based UI adjustments, and permission handling within the Pamflow application.
